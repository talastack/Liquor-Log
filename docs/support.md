# Support

*Pour Memo, by TALASTACK LLC. Last updated 22 September 2026.*

Pour Memo is a bourbon and spirits collection and tasting journal. It keeps
track of what you own, what is left in each bottle, what you thought of it,
and whether you already have the one in your hand.

## Getting help

Write to **privacy@talastack.com**. It is one mailbox for questions,
problems and anything about your data.

So that an answer does not take two rounds, say what you tapped, what you
expected and what happened instead, and which iPhone and which version of
iOS. If a number looks wrong, the bottle it is on helps more than a
screenshot of the whole screen.

## The things people ask first

**Where is my collection kept?** On your phone. The app works with no
connection at all, which is the normal case in a liquor store rather than
an error. Signing in is optional and only exists so a second device, or a
partner, can see the same shelf.

**How do I get my data out?** More -> Your data -> Export everything as
CSV. You get plain files: the collection, tastings, pours, the hunt log and
distillery visits. They are yours; nothing about them is specific to this
app.

**How do I delete my account?** More -> Sync -> Delete my account. The
account and every row belonging to it are removed from the server. Deleting
the app removes the copy on the phone.

**Why does a bottle say a fact is unchecked?** Because it is. A bottle
whose facts came from the bundled catalogue says where they came from, and
says plainly when nobody has checked them against a published source. A
starting point is more useful than a confident wrong answer.

**Is there a subscription?** No. There is nothing to buy, no advertising
and no third-party analytics.
