# Privacy policy

*Liquor-Log, by TALASTACK LLC. Effective 16 September 2026.*

This is the whole policy. It is short because the app keeps almost nothing about you.

## What the app stores, and where

**Your collection stays on your phone.** Bottles, pours, tastings, notes, photos, wishlist, prices you paid — all of it is stored in a database on your device. The app works completely without an internet connection and does not need an account.

**Photos** you take of bottles are stored on your device and never uploaded. When you scan a label, the text is read on the phone by Apple's built-in recognition; the image is not sent anywhere.

**Exports and backups** are files the app creates for you (CSV, PDF, a backup file). Where you send them is up to you.

## If you create an account (optional)

Sync is optional and exists only so the same collection can be on a second device. If you turn it on, you create an account with an email address and password. Then:

- Your collection is copied to a server we run on Supabase, in the United States, under your account, and protected so that only you — and anyone you deliberately share a household with — can read it.
- Your password is never stored by us in readable form. Your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.
- Your email address is used to sign you in and for nothing else. We do not send marketing email.
- **Sharing with a partner** is something you do on purpose, by creating a household and giving someone its code. Both of you then see each other's collection. Leaving the household stops that.
- Deleting your account removes your data from the server. Sign out and delete the app to remove it from the device.

## If you turn on "Share what you see" (optional, off by default)

With this switched on, two things you record are sent as anonymous reports: the shelf prices you type and the wax-drip measurements you make. Each report carries the product, the figure, the date and — only if you entered it — your state. It does not carry your name, email, account id or location. Other users see only totals: how many reports, and a median. Switching sharing off withdraws every report you sent.

## If you publish a menu (Pro)

"What's open" can be published as a web page at an address the app generates. Anyone with the link can see the menu — bottle names only, no prices — until you take it down.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, no ad identifiers.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that phone home.
- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
- No location access.
- No estimate of how much you have drunk, and nothing that measures intoxication.

## Purchases

Pro is a subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

## Children

The app is for adults of legal drinking age and is rated accordingly on the App Store.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at the same address with a new effective date. Changes that would let us do more with your data will be announced in the app before they take effect.
